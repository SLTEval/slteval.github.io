Beyond Proxy Metrics: Rethinking Evaluation for Speech and Language Technologies

Strong scores do not always reflect real-world quality. Most of the world's languages have sparse data and little dedicated evaluation infrastructure. Existing benchmarks often miss disfluencies, code-mixing, morphological richness, dialectal variation, and spontaneous input. Optimizing proxy metrics such as WER, BLEU, ROUGE, and MOS can obscure whether a system understands, generates, or interacts meaningfully. Speech-to-speech translation, automatic dubbing, and atypical speech also need reliable evaluation methods.

Organized by the Language Technologies Research Centre (LTRC), IIIT Hyderabad, SLT-Eval brings together researchers across ASR, MT, TTS, speech and video translation, LLMs, and multimodal systems to build standardized, interpretable, human-aligned metrics, benchmarks, datasets, and shared tasks. We welcome work on all languages and resource settings.

SLT-Eval's key research questions:
  • How can evaluation move beyond proxy metrics toward meaningful linguistic, perceptual, and real-world quality?
  • How can evaluation generalize across languages, dialects, domains, and resource conditions?
  • How can automatic metrics be validated against human judgments while accounting for robustness, fairness, and linguistic diversity?
