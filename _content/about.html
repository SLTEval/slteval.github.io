Why this workshop

Quality is more than a leaderboard.

Strong scores do not always reflect real-world quality. Most of the world’s languages have sparse data and little dedicated evaluation infrastructure. Benchmarks often miss disfluencies, code-mixing, morphological richness, dialectal variation, and spontaneous input.

Optimizing proxy metrics such as WER, BLEU, ROUGE, and MOS can obscure whether a system understands, generates, or interacts meaningfully. Speech-to-speech translation, automatic dubbing, and atypical speech also need reliable evaluation methods.

Organized by the Language Technologies Research Centre (LTRC), IIIT Hyderabad, SLT-Eval brings together researchers across ASR, MT, TTS, speech and video translation, LLMs, and multimodal systems to build standardized, interpretable, human-aligned metrics, benchmarks, datasets, and shared tasks.

We welcome work on all languages and resource settings.
