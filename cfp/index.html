Call for Papers

Original research, new resources, and fresh perspectives on evaluation.

SLT-Eval 2027 is a hybrid event, with in-person and remote participation.

We invite original research papers, position papers, resource papers, and system demonstrations addressing evaluation challenges across speech and language technologies.

New metrics, datasets, critiques, and negative results are welcome, in any language. All accepted papers, including shared task system description papers, will be presented at the workshop and published in the COLING 2027 workshop proceedings, available in the ACL Anthology.

Paper types & formatting

Long papers

Up to 8 pages

Short papers

Up to 4 pages

References and appendices do not count toward page limits.

Double-blind review. Main CFP submissions must be anonymized. There will be no rebuttal phase; authors will receive acceptance decisions directly.

Use the official ACL templates and follow the ACL formatting guidelines.

Research scope

Topics of interest

Including, but not limited to:

  • Evaluation methodologies for MT, NLG, LLMs, and multimodal systems
  • Evaluation of ASR, TTS, speech translation, and Speech LLMs
  • Benchmark and dataset design for low-resource and multilingual evaluation
  • Metrics for speech-to-speech translation and automatic dubbing
  • Video-to-video translation and lip-sync evaluation
  • Evaluation of stuttered, dysarthric, and other atypical speech
  • Human vs. automatic evaluation methods
  • Cross-lingual and code-mixed evaluation
  • Bias, robustness, and interpretability in speech and NLP systems
  • Meta-evaluation of metrics and agreement with human judgments
  • LLM-as-a-judge: reliability, bias, and calibration
  • Evaluation of dialects, accents, disfluencies, and noisy or spontaneous speech
