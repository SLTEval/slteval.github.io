About the workshop

Evaluation as a research problem.

A low WER does not guarantee understanding. A high BLEU score does not guarantee a natural translation. Strong benchmarks can still miss the things that matter to people.

We bring together researchers in speech, language, and multimodal systems to build evaluation that is interpretable, reproducible, and aligned with human judgment.
